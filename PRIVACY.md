# Privacy Policy: Pumperly for iOS

Pumperly for iOS shows the Pumperly website, https://pumperly.com, and adds a home screen widget. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell data.

## What the app uses

- **Location.** With your permission, the app reads your device location and gives it to the Pumperly page you are looking at, the same way a browser does, so the page can show stations near you and plan routes. Only pumperly.com pages can receive it.
- **The "Cheapest nearby" widget.** To show the cheapest stations around you, the widget sends your location, rounded to about 110 m, together with your chosen fuel to Pumperly's stations API. The request carries no identifier. Nothing ties it to you, and it is not stored with any identity.
- **On your device only.** The fuel you choose for the widget and the last rounded position the app saw are stored on your iPhone, in storage shared only by the app and its widget. They never leave the device except as the widget request above.

## What the website does

Everything you do inside the app happens on pumperly.com and follows the website's privacy policy, which is shown in the site's menu under Privacy. In short: route queries are sent to compute routes and are not logged or tied to anyone, map tiles and place search use OpenStreetMap-based services, and the only cookie remembers your language.

## What we do not do

- No account, sign-in or registration.
- No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites.
- No sale or sharing of data with data brokers.
